Superelevation
==============

Specifies the superelevation as a ratio measure (slope) at the location of the event.

Positive superelevation is used for curves to the right and negative for curves to the left.
This is consistent with _IfcProfileDef_ coordinate system (as depicted below for _IfcOpenCrossProfileDef_).
