# PEnum_SealType
<!-- end of short definition -->

## Items

### COMPRESSIBLE
Seal that is compressed into joint and expands to provide seal.

### HYDRO_EXPANDING
Hydrophilic swellable profile that swells in contact with water to provide seal.

### MIXED
Hybrid seal combining compressive and hydrophilic components.

### MEMBRANE
Flexible membrane that bridges a gap to provide seal.

### INFLATABLE
Seal installed in gap and inflated to provide seal.
