<!-- end of short definition -->

## Items

### SUMPPUMP
Utilization of pumps to remove water collected in a sump or low point.

### WELLPOINT
System of closely spaced wells connected to a common header.

### DEEPWELL
Utilization of drilled wells to lower the water table in deeper soil layers.

### VACUUMWELLPOINTS
Wellpoint system augmented by vacuum technology for increased dewatering efficiency.

### EDUCTORWELLS
Usage of eductor pumps to induce flow and lift water for dewatering.
