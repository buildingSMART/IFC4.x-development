## Items

### INJECTIONHOSE
Flexible conduit for conveying grout from the injection source to the targeted area, enabling precise and controlled grout placement.

### INJECTIONTUBE
Rigid or semi-rigid tube designed for delivering grout into specific locations, facilitating accurate injection, particularly in confined spaces.

### PETROLEUMPIPE
Pipe adapted for grouting purposes, resistant to substances in the grout, commonly used in scenarios requiring compatibility with petroleum-based grouting materials.

### TUBESAMANCHETTE
Grouting tube equipped with sleeves for controlled injections, providing a method for staged or zonal grouting, enhancing precision in treatment.
